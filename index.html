About

Finance, reporting and assurance with a business perspective.

Financial reporting and corporate accounting professional with over two decades of experience across financial reporting, audit, governance and finance transformation, including a Big Four audit background and long-term leadership experience in a large, complex corporate environment.

As Head of Financial Reporting, Methodology & Data Management at NIS a.d. Novi Sad, I work at the intersection of IFRS, accounting methodology, audit requirements, business operations and data governance. My role combines technical financial reporting with leadership, management reporting, financial controls and the development of consistent methodologies and reliable data frameworks that support both external reporting and management decision-making.

My professional experience includes complex IFRS matters, business combinations, impairment testing, significant accounting estimates and judgments, group reporting, audit coordination and financial control environments. I have also been actively involved in finance transformation, process optimisation, system implementation, SAP BPC reporting, digitalisation, RPA and KPI-based performance management.

Earlier in my career, I worked in Big Four audit at Deloitte across manufacturing, banking and insurance, following an initial finance leadership role in the public sector.
